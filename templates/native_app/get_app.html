⬇️ Download the Android app

  1. Tap Download the Android app. If Chrome asks whether to keep the file, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file, diction-masters.apk (from the notification, or your Downloads folder).
  3. If your phone says it can't install unknown apps, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open. If Play Protect asks, choose Install anyway.

New version out? Download it the same way and install it over the old one — your account and downloaded lessons stay.

⬇️ Download the Android app

  1. Tap Download the Android app. If Chrome asks whether to keep the file, tap Download anyway.

Install Diction Masters on your computer. It opens in its own window, works offline with downloaded lessons, and updates itself.

🪟 Windows

⬇️ Windows 10 and 11

Windows 7, 8 and 8.1 (32- or 64-bit)

  1. Open the downloaded file.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Click Install. Diction Masters appears on your desktop and in the Start menu.

🍎 Mac

⬇️ Mac with Apple chip (M1 or newer)

Mac with Intel chip (macOS 12 or newer) · older Macs (macOS 10.13–11)

Not sure? Apple menu → About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M…" or "Processor: Intel".

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Diction Masters into Applications.
  2. Open it from Applications. If your Mac says it can't check the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

🐧 Linux

⬇️ Ubuntu, Debian, Mint (.deb)

Any other Linux (AppImage)

  1. .deb: double-click it to install, or run sudo apt install ./DictionMasters-linux-amd64.deb.
  2. AppImage: right-click it → Properties → allow running as a program, then double-click it.

New version out? The app updates itself. Your account and downloaded lessons stay.

{% comment %} The face that reads with the learner. A tutor with a portrait uploaded (a 3D character picture, say) shows that; otherwise the drawn face for its style is used. Either way it comes alive while the tutor speaks — the drawn mouth moves, a portrait breathes and glows — from static/js/tutor_avatar.js, which adds "is-speaking". Pass `avatar` (the drawn style), `portrait` (an image address or ""), `name`, `size` in pixels, and `suffix` to keep gradients apart when several faces are on one page. {% endcomment %}

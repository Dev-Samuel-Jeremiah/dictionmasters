Read aloud. Get coached as you go.

Choose a passage and read it out loud, one sentence at a time. Your tutor listens. If a word doesn't come out right, it stops, says the word properly and lets you try it. When you finish, you get your reading level and feedback straight away.

British English only. Every word is modelled in Received Pronunciation — the accent on the phonemic chart — and the words Americans say differently are practised with you as you read.

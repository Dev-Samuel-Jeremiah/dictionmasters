Where this works

  • Android app: from the next app version (the one with this switch). Older copies of the app block everyone.
  • Computer app (Windows and Mac): from its next version, which installs itself. Linux can't block screenshots at all.
  • iPhone app and web browsers: they can't block screenshots, so nothing changes there.
  • Offline computer app (flash drives): always blocked. It never connects to the website, so this switch can't reach it.

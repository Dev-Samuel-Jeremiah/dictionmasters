Upload school details

Upload the supplied school workbook or use the template. The importer reads visible sheets, merges repeated school names across tabs, and accepts blank email addresses.

  • Required: school name. Optional: location, contact person, phone, school contact email and New/Returning status.
  • Each new school gets a school admin login. The contact person's name becomes their username; when it is blank, a school-based admin username is created.
  • A short 8-character password is generated automatically from one simple word and five random digits. Usernames and passwords download in a private Excel login sheet as soon as registration completes.
  • Every row is checked before anything is saved. If a row has an error, no schools are registered.
  • Schools already in the system are skipped without changing their details or logins. Their current credentials remain available from the school people page.
  • Admins sign in with the downloaded username and password. The school contact email is saved for reference and is not used as the login.

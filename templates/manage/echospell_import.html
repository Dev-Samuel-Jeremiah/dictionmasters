Upload one level of the book

A PDF or Word file of a whole level. Every LEVEL X GROUP Y heading in it becomes a group, and that group's pages fill in its cards:

  • the numbered word list → the Spelling card
  • the story under it, with its title → the Passage reading card
  • Conversation Group Y, line by line with each speaker → the Dialogue card
  • Vocabulary Group Y is left out, and so is everything before the first group heading

You'll see everything that was found, and what it would change, before anything is saved. Recordings already uploaded are never touched.

Install Diction Masters

Open it from your home screen like any app, full screen, and always up to date.

  1. Tap the Share button ⬆️ at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.
  1. Tap the browser menu ⋮ at the top right.
  2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install.
  1. In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon ⊞ at the right of the address bar.
  2. Or open the browser menu and choose Install Diction Masters.
  3. On a Mac with Safari, choose File → Add to Dock.
